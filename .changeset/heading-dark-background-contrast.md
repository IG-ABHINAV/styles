New `pathable-heading--on-dark` modifier draws a `pathable-heading` in white (12.48:1 on PathAble Blue) for use on a dark background such as `pathable-dark-background`, where the default heading colour is the container colour (1.00:1). The React `Heading` takes it as the `onDark` prop. A long word in the heading wraps inside its container instead of running past it. Without the modifier a heading is unchanged.
